## EVIDENCE

List only governed Evidence. For each item record `evidence_id`, `source_id`, direct meaning, transmission-path position, timing, and the uncertainty it reduces. Preserve Evidence ID → Source ID → original excerpt → locator trace.

## COUNTEREVIDENCE

List contradictory, weakening, delayed, or scope-mismatched Evidence IDs. State whether each conflict is open, resolved, or `KNOWN_UNKNOWN`; do not silently discard it.

## COMPETITOR / ECOSYSTEM CONTEXT

State only context that can change relative opportunity, qualification urgency, platform timing, or dependency risk. Separate direct evidence from interpretation.

## CONSTRAINT / POLICY CONTEXT

Identify any credible path from a production, material, logistics, macro, or policy condition to the decision. If the marginal constraint or transmission path is not established, record `KNOWN_UNKNOWN` rather than adding generic context.

## KNOWN UNKNOWNS

List the internal or unavailable facts required for a stronger conclusion, such as customer volume, price, share, yield, cost, CAPA, or allocation. Do not estimate them without approved evidence.
